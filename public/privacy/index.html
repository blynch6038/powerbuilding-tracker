Power Builder privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy covers the Power Builder iPhone app and the web tracker at power.lynchnetwork.com. Power Builder provides invitation-only workout logging and progress tracking. For privacy questions, contact info@adaptivedigitalgroup.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, and authentication information needed to provide sign-in and account access. Password authentication is handled by Supabase; passwords are not stored in workout records.
  • Workout information you enter: exercises, program weeks and training days, sets, weights, repetitions, timed or distance-based entries where applicable, RPE, completion status, exercise substitutions, and notes, including machine starting weights. Records can include creation or update timestamps and history notes.
  • Preferences and device storage: selected workout, exercise order, session information, cached workout records, and edits waiting to synchronize.
  • Support information: your email and information you choose to include when contacting us.
  • Service information: hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, browser or device details, and security or error logs to operate and protect the service. If you choose to share TestFlight feedback or diagnostics, Apple may make that information available to us.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, save and synchronize workouts, calculate training recommendations and progress, restore preferences, resolve conflicts, respond to requests, and maintain service security. Recommendations and estimated maximum lifts are calculated from workout records. Power Builder does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and service providers

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud database storage for synchronized account and workout data. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Apple provides app distribution, TestFlight, and system features such as the rest-timer Live Activity. These providers process information needed to deliver their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The iPhone app keeps workout data and pending edits on your device and stores sign-in session credentials in Apple's Keychain. The web app uses browser storage, including IndexedDB and local storage, for offline records, session information, and preferences. Network connections to the backend use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Service providers may process data outside your country. Their information is available at Supabase Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement, and Apple Privacy Policy.

Sharing and tracking

Workout history is associated with your account and is not published as a public feed. Authorized service administrators may access information as needed to operate the service and handle support. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users. Power Builder does not include advertising trackers or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. It does not currently read Apple Health data, contacts, or location.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and workout information while your account remains active to provide your history and synchronization. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. We verify ownership, remove the account and associated data from active systems, and retain only information necessary for legal obligations, security, or resolving disputes. Deleted records may remain in provider backups until those backups expire under the provider's retention practices.

Local copies and exported backups may remain on your devices after cloud deletion. After your deletion request is completed, remove local app or browser data and any backups you no longer want. Deleting the app alone does not delete your cloud account. Some edits and deletion markers may remain in synchronization history until account deletion.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can review and edit workout entries and export a backup using the app's available controls. To request access, correction, an export, account deletion, or to withdraw permission for continued processing based on consent, email info@adaptivedigitalgroup.com. Use your account email where possible; do not send passwords. We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership. Withdrawing information needed to operate an account may require closing that account.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, or the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

Children

Power Builder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the effective date here. Material changes will be communicated through the app or account contact information where appropriate. Contact info@adaptivedigitalgroup.com or visit Support with questions.
