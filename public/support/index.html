Power Builder support

Help with your training app.

Get help with the Power Builder iPhone app and web tracker. Access to workout accounts is invitation-only.

Contact support

Email info@adaptivedigitalgroup.com for account access, app issues, feedback, or feature requests.

Email support

What to include

  • Your app version and build number, or the browser you use.
  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • What happened, what you expected, and steps to reproduce it.
  • The affected program week, training day, and exercise, if relevant.

Never send your password, authentication codes, or session tokens. Remove private information from screenshots. Only include workout details needed to explain the issue.

Sign-in and invitations

Use the same invited account on the iPhone app and website. To manage or reset your password, open Power Builder on the web and use the account or password-reset controls. Contact support if you need an invitation or cannot access your account.

Missing history or synchronization issues

Entries can be saved locally while offline and uploaded when a connection is available. Reconnect, keep the app open, and use Settings → Sync now. Check for pending edits or conflicts and confirm you are signed into the correct account.

Export a backup before clearing browser storage or reinstalling the app. Unsynchronized changes may exist only on your device. If records still appear missing, contact support before deleting or re-entering them.

Privacy and account deletion

To request access, correction, an export, or deletion of your account and associated workout data, email info@adaptivedigitalgroup.com, preferably from your account email address. We may verify account ownership before acting. See our Privacy Policy for details.
